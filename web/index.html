Theoretical Architecture & Literature Alignment (DA1)

Paper 1 (IJCAI 2025)

Conditional Diffusion (CoFILL)

Uses dual-stream temporal and frequency denoising process conditioned on observed points to mitigate cumulative error. Benchmarked as a comparative baseline in our framework.

Proposed Solution

Spatio-Temporal Transformer (Ours)

Combines multi-head self-attention with diurnal harmonic positional encodings and learnable [MASK] tokens (inspired by CT-MVP, ESFM, and TabImpute), achieving the strictly lowest MAE across all missingness regimes.

Objective 3

Calibrated Epistemic Uncertainty (MC Dropout)

By sampling posterior predictive weights via test-time Monte Carlo Dropout, the framework derives calibrated 95% confidence bands, attaching trustworthiness scores to imputed values before downstream forecasting.
